Descripción

Texto original de la comunidad de AniList, en inglés y sin traducir.

Species: Chimera Ant Rank: Squad Captain Squad: Hagya's Squad Chimera ant with the appearance and personality of a young girl. Usually seen accompanying Leol and Flutter. Previously named "Hirin (ヒリン)".

Contenido marcado como spoiler en AniList (1)

Hina is later revealed to be a nen exorcist; she is capable of removing curses or any other sort of inconveniences caused by another nen user. Hina's belly expands with the aura she has taken, causing her to appear almost pregnant. After using the ability, her movement is limited.
