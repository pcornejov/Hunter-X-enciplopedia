Shizuku Murasaki

シズク

Voz japonesa

  • Miho Arakawa 荒川美穂

Descripción

Texto original de la comunidad de AniList, en inglés y sin traducir.

Shizuku is member number 8 of the Phantom Troupe. She has been described as being a natural airhead, seeing as how some of her actions do not point towards her being very bright. Shizuku is quite forgetful; once she forgets something, she won't remember it. Her opponents are often fooled by her unassuming and blurred appearance—but this has often proved to be fatal for them once she enters in combat. Shizuku is also left-handed.

Nen Ability: Deme-chan (Blinky): Shizuku uses her nen to create a self-styled "living" vacuum cleaner she names "Deme-chan" that is able to consume any inanimate matter under her command. This has proven very useful in many occasions for cleaning up evidence that the members leave behind, especially blood and dead corpses. She also uses the razor-sharp teeth on the "mouth" as a weapon when in combat. Anything sucked up into her vacuum cleaner cannot be retrieved, except for the last material that was sucked in.
